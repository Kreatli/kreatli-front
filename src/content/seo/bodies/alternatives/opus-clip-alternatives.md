# Opus Clip Alternatives: Other AI Clippers or a Human Clipper

**The main Opus Clip alternatives are other AI clipping apps, such as Submagic, Vizard, Klap or CapCut, or hiring a human clipper who picks the moments and edits each clip by hand.** OpusClip (often searched as "Opus Clip") is fast and cheap: as of October 2026 it has a free plan and paid plans from $15 a month. People look elsewhere when the AI picks the wrong moments, the hooks feel generic, or they spend too long fixing clips. Many creators end up combining an AI app with a clipper.

This page covers what OpusClip does, what it costs, why people switch, and how each alternative compares.

## What OpusClip does

OpusClip is an AI app that turns a long video into short vertical clips. You upload a podcast, stream VOD or YouTube video, or paste a link. The app then:

- **Picks the moments** it thinks will work as clips
- **Reframes them to 9:16** so they fill a phone screen
- **Adds animated captions**, emoji and keyword highlights
- **Writes an auto hook** for the start of each clip
- Lets you **export** clips for TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Reels

It's built for creators who want a lot of clips quickly, without editing by hand.

## How much does OpusClip cost?

We checked [OpusClip's pricing page](https://www.opus.pro/pricing) in October 2026. It lists four plans:

| Plan | Price | What stands out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0, no card needed | Watermark on animated captions, exports kept for 3 days, up to 1080p, 1 brand template |
| Starter | $15 per month | No watermark, highest export quality, files kept for 29 days, 1 brand template |
| Pro | $29 per month | No watermark, 100GB storage, up to 4 brand templates, team seats |

All plans offer clip lengths from under a minute up to 15 minutes. Prices and limits change often, so check the pricing page before you sign up. The subscription is the small part of the cost. The bigger cost is your time: someone still has to review, fix and post every clip.

## Why people look for OpusClip alternatives

OpusClip works well for many creators. These are the most common reasons people look elsewhere:

- **It picks the wrong moments.** Software looks for signals like energy, keywords and faces. It can miss the line your audience cares about, or cut a joke before the punchline.
- **The hooks feel generic.** Auto hooks follow patterns. After a few weeks, clips can start to look like everyone else's.
- **Captions and style.** Preset caption styles are fast, but matching your fonts, colors and pacing on every clip takes manual work.
- **Watermarks and limits on the free plan.** Free exports carry a watermark on animated captions and expire after 3 days.
- **Review time.** At high volume, checking and fixing every clip becomes a job of its own.

## The alternatives

### Other AI clipping apps

These apps work like OpusClip: a subscription, and software that does the first pass. Switching can help if you want a different caption style or workflow, but the limits above mostly stay the same.

- **Submagic:** best known for animated captions and quick edits on short videos.
- **Vizard:** turns long videos into draft clips, similar to OpusClip.
- **Klap:** turns YouTube videos into shorts automatically.
- **CapCut:** a free editor with auto captions. You pick and cut the moments yourself, so it takes more time but gives you full control.

We don't rank these apps or list their prices, because both change often. Check each app's own pricing page.

### Hire a human clipper

A clipper watches your content, picks the moments, and edits each clip by hand. That covers the hook, captions in your style, pacing, B-roll and safe zones. You get finished clips to post on your own channels.

Freelance clips typically cost **$15 to $35** each for simple talking-head or podcast edits, **$30 to $50** for mid-level work, and **$50 to $75 or more** for complex gaming or motion work. Monthly retainers run about $800 to $3,500 or more. See [clipper pay rates](/guides/clipper-pay-rates) for the full breakdown and [how much to pay clippers](/guides/how-much-to-pay-clippers) for budgets.

On Kreatli Marketplace, you post a job with the clips you need, and clippers apply. You agree pay and terms directly with the clipper. Kreatli doesn't process payments.

### Use both

Many creators combine the two. An AI app finds candidate moments, and a clipper chooses the best ones and finishes them. Many clippers already use AI tools to speed up first cuts. You pay for finished clips, and the clipper decides what's worth posting.

**Worked example:** a podcast posts 12 clips a week, or about 48 a month. OpusClip Pro costs $29 a month, but someone still spends hours reviewing and fixing clips. A clipper at $25 per simple clip costs about $1,200 a month for the same 48 clips, with no review time on your side. Many creators split it: AI for everyday clips, and a clipper for the 10 to 20 clips a month that matter most.

## When AI is enough, and when to hire

**An AI app is often enough when:**

- You're testing short-form and don't want to spend much yet
- Your content is simple talking-head video with clear, quotable moments
- You have time to review and fix clips yourself

**Hire a clipper when:**

- Short-form is a real growth channel and you post several clips a week
- Clips need judgement, such as gaming moments, humor or stories that need context
- You want a consistent style without fixing auto-generated clips

## FAQ

### How much does OpusClip cost?

As of October 2026, OpusClip has a free plan, Starter at $15 per month, Pro at $29 per month and a Business plan with custom pricing. Paid plans remove the watermark and keep your files longer. Check opus.pro/pricing for current prices before you sign up.

### Is OpusClip free?

OpusClip has a free plan that doesn't need a card. As of October 2026, free exports carry a watermark on animated captions, are kept for 3 days and are limited to 1080p. To export without a watermark, you need a paid plan.

### Is OpusClip worth it?

OpusClip is worth it if you need a lot of clips fast and have time to review them. It's less useful if your clips need careful moment picking, humor or a consistent brand style, because you'll spend that time fixing clips. Try the free plan on one real video before you pay.

### What is the best free OpusClip alternative?

CapCut is the most common free option. It has auto captions, but you pick and cut the moments yourself. If you want the AI to pick moments for free, most AI clipping apps have a free tier with limits. Check each app's current plan.

### Is OpusClip better than hiring a clipper?

It depends on what you need. OpusClip is much cheaper per clip and faster. A human clipper picks moments with more judgement and matches your style, but costs about $15 to $75 or more per clip. Many creators use OpusClip for volume and a clipper for their most important clips.

### Can I use OpusClip and a clipper together?

Yes. A common setup is to run long videos through an AI app to find candidate moments, then have a clipper pick the best ones and finish them with hooks, captions and pacing. Many clippers already use AI tools for first cuts.
