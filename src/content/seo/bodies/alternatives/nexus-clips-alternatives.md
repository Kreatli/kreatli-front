# Nexus Clips Alternatives and Pricing: Other AI Apps or a Human Clipper

**Nexus Clips doesn't publish its prices. As of November 2026, its website shows a free account that needs no card and two paid plans, Clips and Creator, billed monthly or yearly, and you only see the price once you sign in.** The main Nexus Clips alternatives are other AI clipping apps with public pricing, such as OpusClip, or hiring a human clipper who picks the moments and edits each clip in your style. Many creators combine the two.

## How much does Nexus Clips cost?

Nexus Clips has a free account and two paid plans, but the website doesn't show what the paid plans cost. We checked nexusclips.com on 26 November 2026. Its public pages describe the plans but leave the prices out, so you see the amount only after you sign in.

| Plan | Who it's for (per the Nexus Clips site) | What it includes | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free account | Trying the app | Sign up with no card. The site doesn't list the free limits. | $0 |
| Clips | Podcasts, streams, webinars and long YouTube videos | Finds the best moments, edits each clip, animated captions, vertical reframing, a hook and outro, silence removal, your own voiceover, and posting to TikTok and YouTube | Shown in the app |
| Creator | People who shoot native content, mostly talking to camera | Everything in Clips, plus motion graphics, B-roll, sound effects, music, visual effects, transitions, a video recorder and an AI script | Shown in the app |

A few more details from the site:

- **Monthly or yearly billing.** Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime. Yearly billing comes with a discount.
- **Usage limits.** Each plan includes a monthly amount of AI usage. Finding moments, captions, templates and AI edit styles count towards it. Manual edits on a finished project don't. Unused usage doesn't roll over, and the site says the service "is not unlimited AI processing."
- **Free vs. paid downloads.** The site lists HD downloads without a watermark as a paid feature, so expect a watermark on the free account.
- **Refunds.** You can ask for a refund within 7 days of your first paid charge, but only if you haven't used the service since. Renewals aren't refunded for a change of mind.
- **Cancelling.** You cancel from Profile, then Settings, then Subscription plan. Paid access lasts until the end of the period you paid for.

**How to see the price:** create the free account, then open the plan page in the app. If you're comparing costs, write down the monthly price, the yearly price and the monthly usage on each plan. Usage is what decides how many videos you can actually clip.

For comparison, OpusClip publishes its prices. As of October 2026 it listed a free plan, Starter at $15 per month and Pro at $29 per month. See [Opus Clip alternatives](/alternatives/opus-clip-alternatives) for the full plan table.

## What Nexus Clips does

Nexus Clips is an AI app that turns long videos into short vertical clips. As of November 2026, its website says it:

- **Finds the best moments** in a podcast, stream or long video and cuts them into clips
- **Frames each clip on the speaker's face** and **adds captions**, with a choice of caption styles
- Imports from **YouTube or Twitch** by link, or from a file on your computer
- Exports clips for **TikTok, Reels and YouTube Shorts**
- On paid plans, **posts directly to TikTok and YouTube**

You tell it what to look for, pick a caption style and say where you'll post. Then you review the clips, fix what you need in its editor, and download or publish them.

## Nexus Clips review: strengths and limits

This is our read of what Nexus Clips offers based on its website, not a hands-on test.

**Strengths:**

- **Fast and simple.** Paste a link, pick a style, and you get draft clips in minutes.
- **Built for talking content.** Face framing, captions and silence removal suit podcasts, streams and talking-head video.
- **Posting built in.** Paid plans can publish to TikTok and YouTube without downloading first.
- **Low-risk to try.** The free account needs no card.

**Limits:**

- **No public pricing.** You can't budget before you sign up, and you can't compare it side by side with apps that publish prices.
- **Usage caps.** Paid plans have monthly AI usage, so heavy uploaders need to check the allowance, not just the price.
- **The AI picks the moments.** Like every AI clipper, it looks for signals like energy, keywords and faces. It can miss the line your audience cares about most.
- **Preset styles.** Caption styles and templates are a starting point. Matching your brand on every clip still takes manual work.
- **Strict refunds.** After the first 7 days, or once you've used the service, refunds are generally not offered.

## Free Nexus Clips alternatives

There's no fully free way to get finished clips at volume, but these options cost little or nothing to try:

- **OpusClip's free plan.** As of October 2026, it needed no card. Free exports carry a watermark on animated captions, are kept for 3 days and are limited to 1080p.
- **Free tries from other AI apps.** Many AI clipping apps let you try them free, with limits, but not all have a free plan. Submagic, for example, offers a free try but no free plan (see [Submagic alternatives](/alternatives/submagic-alternatives)). Check each app's current plan page, because free limits change often.
- **CapCut.** A free editor with auto captions. You pick and cut the moments yourself, so it takes longer but gives you full control.

If your time is the real cost, a free app may not be the cheapest option. An hour a day fixing AI clips adds up fast.

## The alternatives

### Other AI clipping apps

**OpusClip** is the best-known AI clipping app and publishes its prices. **Vizard** and **Klap** work in a similar way, turning long videos into draft shorts for a monthly subscription. **Submagic** focuses more on captions and quick edits. Switching apps can get you public pricing, a different free tier or different features. The limits of AI moment picking stay largely the same.

### Hire a human clipper

A clipper watches your content, picks the moments and edits each clip by hand: the hook, captions in your style, pacing, B-roll and safe zones. You get finished clips to post on your own channels. On Kreatli Marketplace, you post a job with the clips you need, and clippers apply. You agree the pay with the clipper upfront.

Freelance clips typically cost **$15 to $35** each for simple talking-head or podcast edits, **$30 to $50** for mid-level work, and **$50 to $75 or more** for complex gaming or motion work. Monthly retainers run about $800 to $3,500 or more. See [clipper pay rates](/guides/clipper-pay-rates) for the breakdown and [how much to pay clippers](/guides/how-much-to-pay-clippers) for sample budgets.

**Worked example:** you want 20 podcast clips a month. At $20 to $30 per clip, a clipper costs about $400 to $600 a month. An AI app costs a monthly subscription, plus the hours you spend reviewing and fixing its clips.

### Combine AI and a human clipper

Many creators use both. An AI app surfaces candidate moments, and a clipper chooses the best ones and finishes them. Many clippers already use AI tools to speed up first cuts. What you pay for is the finished clip.

## When AI is enough, and when to hire

**An AI app is often enough when:**

- You're testing short-form and don't want to spend much yet
- Your content is simple talking-head video with clear, quotable moments
- You have time to review and fix clips yourself

**Hire a clipper when:**

- Short-form is a real growth channel and you post several clips a week
- Clips need judgement, such as gaming moments, humor or context-heavy stories
- You want a consistent style without reviewing every auto-generated clip
