## What does a TikTok clipper do?

A TikTok clipper turns your raw footage (UGC, podcast video, product demos, lifestyle B-roll, stream highlights) into platform-native short videos. TikTok-specialist clippers know:

- **Hook density**: a visual or verbal payoff in the first 1–2 seconds, not 5 ([more on hooks](/glossary/video-hook-first-3-seconds))
- **Trend literacy**: when to use trending formats, text overlays, and stitch- or duet-friendly framing, without adding audio you cannot license
- **Caption culture**: on-screen text that works muted, with line breaks that match TikTok norms ([burned-in captions explained](/glossary/burned-in-captions-meaning))
- **Loop-friendly endings**: cuts that encourage rewatches
- **Batch variation**: the same source cut with multiple hooks for testing

This role differs from a "TikTok manager" who might also handle strategy and posting. On Kreatli Marketplace, you are hiring **clipping and editing work**, so say clearly in your brief if you also need upload or scheduling help.

## What to include in your TikTok clipping job post

The fastest way to attract the right TikTok clippers is a specific job post. Include:

- **Your niche**: coach, ecommerce brand, streamer, podcaster, local business, or creator
- **Source footage**: long-form videos, raw phone footage, streams, or product shoots
- **Posting frequency**: how many TikToks per week you want to publish
- **Style references**: 2–3 TikTok accounts whose editing style you want to emulate, plus one you do not like
- **Caption rules**: font, color, placement, and whether to use emoji
- **Audio rules**: approved sound sources and whether the clipper may suggest trending sounds for you to add in-app
- **Hook variants**: whether you want one version per clip or 2–3 alternate openings
- **Deliverable format**: MP4 only, or project files too
- **Pay and availability**: project-based, part-time, or full-time, and your pay type

## How to hire a TikTok clipper

1. **Post your job brief**: define your niche, posting frequency, and style references.
2. **Review clipper profiles**: look for vertical samples with TikTok-native pacing, not horizontal YouTube compilations.
3. **Run a paid test batch**: 3–5 clips from the same source footage you will use long term.
4. **Agree scope and price**: move to a weekly batch or monthly rate once the style fits.
5. **Receive finished clips**: approve, request revisions, and post from your account.

## How to vet TikTok clippers

TikTok portfolios can look impressive while hiding weak fundamentals. When reviewing samples and test batches, check:

- **Opening frame**: does something happen immediately, or does the clip warm up?
- **Text on screen**: is it readable on a phone and clear of TikTok's buttons and caption area?
- **Cut speed**: fast without being exhausting, with pauses removed but natural speech kept
- **Audio choices**: did they stay within your licensing rules?
- **Variation**: when asked for three hooks, are they genuinely different, or the same opening reworded?
- **Brief adherence**: did they hit the length, caption style, and deadline you asked for?

Ask each applicant which of their clips performed best and why they think it worked. Good clippers can explain their choices.

## FAQ

### Do I need a TikTok clipper if I already cross-post YouTube Shorts?

Shorts and TikTok share a vertical format but have **different cultures**. TikTok clippers often push harder on hooks, on-screen text, and cut speed. If Shorts cross-posts underperform on TikTok, a TikTok-native clipper usually fixes pacing and caption style instead of re-exporting the same file.

### Can clippers use trending sounds on my brand account?

Trending audio can boost reach but often carries **licensing and regional restrictions** for business accounts. Specify in your brief which sound sources are approved, or ask the clipper to suggest sounds that you approve before posting. Responsible clippers flag this upfront.

### How much does it cost to hire clippers for TikTok?

Simple repurposed TikTok clips typically cost **$15–$35 each**, while product edits and complex motion work run higher. Brand accounts posting daily often use monthly retainers instead of per-clip pricing.

### Should I hire one TikTok clipper or a small team?

Solo creators and small brands usually start with **one dedicated clipper** who learns your voice. Teams add more clippers only after a style guide exists. Kreatli Marketplace fits both: post one job for a trial, then expand scope.

No. Content-rewards clipping pays per view on **clipper-owned accounts**. Kreatli Marketplace is **fixed-pay hiring** for clips on **your** TikTok.
