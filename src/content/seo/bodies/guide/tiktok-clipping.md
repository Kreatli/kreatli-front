# TikTok Clipping: How It Works and How to Get Paid

**TikTok clipping** means cutting long videos, such as streams, podcasts and YouTube uploads, into short vertical clips for TikTok. Clippers get paid in two ways. Either a creator hires them to make clips for the creator's own TikTok, for a fixed fee per clip, or they join a CPM campaign and earn per 1,000 views on clips they post on their own account. Fixed-pay clips usually earn **$15 to $35 each** for simple edits. CPM campaigns commonly advertise about **$0.50 to $3 per 1,000 views**.

This guide covers both models, a step-by-step way to make a TikTok clip that holds attention, the permission rules to know, and where to find paid TikTok clipping work.

## The two ways to get paid for TikTok clipping

| | Fixed pay for a creator | CPM campaign |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who posts the clip | The creator, on their TikTok | You, on your own TikTok |
| How you're paid | An agreed price per clip, batch or month | Per 1,000 views on approved clips |
| Typical pay | $15 to $35 per simple clip, $50 to $75+ for complex edits | About $0.50 to $3 per 1,000 views |
| Do you need followers? | No, just good samples | It helps, because reach drives earnings |
| How steady | High: the price is agreed upfront | Low: most clips earn little |

### Fixed pay: clipping for a creator's TikTok

A streamer, podcaster or YouTuber sends you their long videos. You return a batch of finished vertical clips, and they post them on their own TikTok. Example: a podcast pays $25 per clip for 12 clips a week, which is $300 a week or about $1,200 a month. This is how clipping works on [Kreatli Marketplace](/clipping-jobs).

### CPM campaigns: posting on your own TikTok

A brand or creator funds a campaign and sets a rate per 1,000 views. You download the approved footage, post clips on your own TikTok, and get paid for views on clips that pass the campaign's rules. Most campaigns set a minimum view count before a clip is paid and a cap on what one clip can earn.

Example: 10 clips at $1 per 1,000 views with a 2,000-view minimum. Six clips stay under 2,000 views and earn $0. Three get 8,000 views each and earn $24 together. One gets 120,000 views and earns $120. That's $144 for 10 clips. Read [CPM meaning in clipping](/glossary/cpm-meaning-clipping) before you join one.

For the full rate tables, see [clipper pay rates](/guides/clipper-pay-rates).

## How to clip for TikTok, step by step

1. **Get the source.** Use footage you're allowed to use: the creator's own files, or a campaign's approved footage.
2. **Find the moment.** Look for a clear start and a payoff, such as a strong opinion, a funny reaction, a surprising fact or a clutch play. It has to make sense to someone who never saw the full video.
3. **Cut it tight.** Most clips land between 15 and 60 seconds. Remove pauses and filler with [jump cuts](/glossary/jump-cut-definition).
4. **Reframe to 9:16.** Turn the wide video into a tall frame that fills a phone screen. Keep the speaker's face centered. See [vertical video and the 9:16 aspect ratio](/glossary/vertical-video-9-16-aspect-ratio).
5. **Add captions.** Many people scroll with the sound off, so burned-in captions matter. Keep them big, short and easy to read.
6. **Open with the hook.** The first 1 to 3 seconds decide whether people keep watching. Start with the strongest line, not the setup. See [the video hook and the first 3 seconds](/glossary/video-hook-first-3-seconds).
7. **Check the safe zones.** TikTok's buttons, captions and username sit on top of the video. Keep faces and text out of those areas. See [safe zones for TikTok, Reels and Shorts](/glossary/short-form-video-safe-zones).
8. **Export and deliver.** Export in vertical HD (1080 × 1920 is the usual size), name files clearly and send them in one batch.

Most TikTok clippers edit in CapCut, which is free and has auto captions. Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve are common once the work gets more complex. Compare them in [the best video editing software for clippers](/guides/best-video-editing-software-for-clippers).

## What makes a TikTok clip work

- **One idea per clip.** A clip that tries to cover two points usually loses people in the middle.
- **Fast pacing.** Cut every pause. If the shot stays still for long, add a zoom or B-roll.
- **Text on screen.** A short title at the top tells viewers what the clip is about before they decide to scroll.
- **A clean ending.** End on the payoff, not on someone starting the next topic.
- **Consistent style.** For a creator's account, keep the same caption font, colors and framing across every clip.

## Permission and rights

Only clip content you have permission to use. When a creator hires you, they've given you their footage. Campaigns give you approved footage and rules for how to use it. Reposting other people's videos without permission can get your posts taken down. Platforms also tend to push down reposted content that adds nothing new.

Read [do you need permission to clip streams](/guides/do-you-need-permission-to-clip-streams) for the basics. It isn't legal advice.

## FAQ

### Can you get paid to clip on TikTok?

Yes. You can get paid by a creator to make clips for their TikTok, usually $15 to $35 per simple clip, or join a CPM campaign that pays per 1,000 views on clips you post on your own account. Fixed pay is steadier. CPM can pay more on a clip that takes off, but most clips earn little.

### How much does TikTok clipping pay?

Fixed-pay TikTok clipping usually pays $15 to $35 per simple clip and $50 to $75 or more for complex edits, or a monthly retainer. CPM campaigns commonly advertise about $0.50 to $3 per 1,000 views, with minimum view counts and payout caps.

### Do you need permission to clip videos for TikTok?

Yes. Clip only footage you're allowed to use, such as a creator's own files or a campaign's approved footage. Posting other people's content without permission can lead to takedowns.

### Can you start TikTok clipping with a new account?

For fixed-pay work, your followers don't matter. Creators post the clips on their own accounts, so they judge you on your samples. For CPM campaigns, a new account can still get views, but earnings depend on reach, and some campaigns set their own account requirements.

### What app do TikTok clippers use?

Most start with CapCut because it's free and has auto captions. Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve are popular for more complex edits. The best app is the one that lets you deliver clean clips quickly.
