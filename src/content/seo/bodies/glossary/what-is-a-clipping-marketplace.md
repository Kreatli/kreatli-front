# What Is a Clipping Marketplace?

A **clipping marketplace** is a website that connects people who need short video clips with people who make them. The name covers two very different models, and picking the wrong one is one of the most common mistakes creators and editors make.

Some marketplaces pay clippers **per view** through content-rewards campaigns. Others, like **Kreatli Marketplace**, are **video editor marketplaces**, where creators hire freelance clippers for a fixed fee.

## Why it matters for creators and clippers

"Clipping marketplace" sounds specific, but the two models work in completely different ways:

| Model | Who gets paid | How pay is worked out | What gets made |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Content-rewards marketplace | Clippers who post | Views, CPM or campaign rules | Clips on the clipper's own accounts |
| Editor marketplace (Kreatli) | Hired freelance editors | A fixed price per job | Clips for the creator's channel |

Creators who want a steady flow of Shorts need the second model. So do editors who want income they can plan around. Content-rewards marketplaces help with getting views, not with hiring an editor.

## Examples

**Content-rewards clipping marketplace:** A fitness brand puts $2,000 into a reward pool. Clippers join the campaign, edit workout highlights and post them from their own Instagram accounts. They earn based on how many views their clips get. The brand gets reach across many accounts.

**Video editor marketplace:** A business coach posts a job on Kreatli Marketplace: "10 clips per week from my podcast, hook-first style, burned-in captions, deliver by Friday." A vetted clip editor is hired at $35 per clip. The finished files go on the coach's TikTok and LinkedIn. Nobody is paid by views.

Same name, different job.

## FAQ

### Is Fiverr a clipping marketplace?

Fiverr is a **general freelance marketplace** with video editing gigs, including some clipping packages. It isn't built for clipping. Quality varies a lot, and its gig model suits one-off orders better than weekly clips. Kreatli Marketplace is built for creators hiring clippers.

### Can one marketplace do both models?

Some companies offer both, but the way **pay works** stays different. Content rewards pay for views. Editor marketplaces pay for the editing work. Mixing up the terms causes confusion, so always check whether you are hiring an editor or funding a pay-per-view campaign.

### How do I hire a clipper on an editor marketplace?

Post a job that says what you need: the source (podcast, stream, course), how many clips, the format (usually vertical 9:16), the caption style, the deadline and your budget. Review editor profiles, agree on a price and receive finished clips. On Kreatli Marketplace, start at [/jobs/create](/jobs/create).
