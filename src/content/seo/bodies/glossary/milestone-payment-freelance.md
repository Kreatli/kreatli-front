# What Is a Milestone Payment?

A **milestone payment** is a partial payment made when a freelancer completes a defined stage of a project, instead of paying everything up front or everything at the end. In milestone payment freelance arrangements, the project is split into checkpoints, each with its own deliverable, deadline, and amount.

For clip editing, a milestone is usually a batch of finished clips, a week of output, or a specific phase like a test clip followed by the full order.

## Why it matters for creators and clippers

Milestones spread risk so neither side carries too much of it at once:

- **Clippers** get paid as work progresses, not weeks later after the whole project ends
- **Creators** pay only for work they've seen and approved
- **Both sides** get natural check-in points to fix style issues early, before they repeat across 30 clips

They also make larger or longer jobs easier to start. A creator who would hesitate to commit to a month of clips can comfortably agree to the first batch.

On Kreatli Marketplace, creators and clippers agree milestones directly in chat. Kreatli does not hold funds or process payments, so milestones are one of the simplest ways to keep both sides protected. See [what escrow payment means](/glossary/escrow-payment-freelance-meaning) for how this compares to platforms that hold funds.

## How milestone payments work

1. **Define the milestones.** Break the project into stages with clear deliverables, such as "5 approved clips."
2. **Set the amount for each.** Usually proportional to the work in that stage.
3. **Set deadlines and review windows.** For example, delivery by Friday, feedback within 48 hours.
4. **Deliver and review.** The clipper submits; the creator approves or requests revisions within the agreed rounds.
5. **Pay.** The creator pays that milestone using the agreed method before or as the next stage begins.

Write all of this down in your chat before work starts. Our guide on [how to write a video editing job post](/guides/how-to-write-a-clipper-job-post) shows how to set expectations from the first message.

## Common milestone structures for clip work

| Structure | How it works | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Test then volume | Paid test clip, then the full order | First-time hires |
| Per batch | Payment after each batch of clips | Ongoing per-clip work |
| Per week | Payment after each week's output | Regular stream or podcast clipping |
| Deposit + balance | Part up front, rest on delivery | Larger one-off projects |
| Phase-based | Setup (templates, style) then production | Custom motion graphics or branding |

## Milestones vs. other payment approaches

- **Full payment up front:** simplest for the clipper, riskiest for the creator.
- **Full payment on completion:** simplest for the creator, riskiest for the clipper.
- **Milestones:** shared risk, more check-ins, slightly more admin.
- **Retainer:** a fixed recurring amount for ongoing work, often paid monthly. See [gig vs. project vs. retainer](/glossary/gig-vs-project-vs-retainer) for the differences.

Kreatli job posts support hourly, per-project, per-month, and per-year pay types. Milestones fit inside any of them, for example a per-project rate split into three batches.

## Examples

**Podcast clips:** A creator orders 20 clips at a per-clip rate. They agree on four milestones of five clips each, paid via PayPal within two days of approval.

**Stream highlights:** A streamer hires a clipper for weekly highlights at a set monthly rate, paid in two halves: one mid-month and one at month end.

**Branded template build:** A clipper builds a custom caption and title template first (milestone one), then produces ten clips using it (milestone two).

## FAQ

### What is an example of a milestone payment?

A creator hiring a clipper for 15 clips might pay for the first five once they're approved, then the next five, then the last five. Each payment is a milestone. A paid test clip before the main order is also a common first milestone.

### How many milestones should a freelance project have?

Enough that no single payment feels risky to either side. For short clip orders, two or three milestones are usually plenty. For ongoing work, weekly or per-batch milestones work well. Too many milestones just adds admin.

### Does Kreatli Marketplace handle milestone payments?

No. Kreatli does not process payments or hold funds. Creators and clippers agree milestones, amounts, and payment methods directly in chat, and pay each other using the methods listed in the job post. Reviews after a completed job help build trust over time.

### How do I set milestone amounts?

Divide the total price in proportion to the work in each stage. If setup work like custom templates is heavy, that stage can carry a larger share.
