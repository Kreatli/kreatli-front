# Clipping Agency vs Clipping Marketplace: Which Should Creators Use?

A **clipping agency** manages clipping for you: it sources clippers, runs the brief and review, and reports results, usually for a campaign budget or retainer. A clipping marketplace like **Kreatli Marketplace** lets you hire clippers directly and manage the relationship yourself, with no agency layer in between.

Agencies are the better choice for some creators and brands. This page explains how each model works and when each one wins.

## What a clipping agency does

"Clipping agency" covers two different businesses, and it helps to know which one you are talking to.

**1. Campaign agencies (distribution).** These run [clipping campaigns](/glossary/what-is-a-clipping-campaign) on your behalf. The agency writes the brief, recruits clippers from its network, reviews submissions, tracks views, and reports back. Clippers usually post on their own accounts, and pricing is typically tied to verified views. Published minimums tend to be in the thousands of dollars. For example, Lumina lists a **$5,000 minimum per campaign** as of 2026, priced as a custom CPM that covers strategy, clip production, distribution through its creator network, brand-safety review, and reporting.

**2. Production agencies (editing for your channel).** These assign an editor or small team to turn your long videos into clips for your own channels. You pay a package or monthly retainer, and an account manager handles scheduling, revisions, and quality control.

Both sell **managed outcomes**. You are paying for coordination, quality checks, and backup capacity, not only the edits.

## What a clipping marketplace does

On **Kreatli Marketplace**, creators (streamers, YouTubers, podcasters, and brands) hire clippers directly:

- Post a **clipping job** with deliverables, style references, availability, and a pay type: **per project, hourly, monthly, or yearly**.
- Review applications and profiles. Clipper profiles are reviewed by the Kreatli team, but you choose who to hire.
- Work **directly** with your clipper on briefs, feedback, and turnaround.
- Receive finished files and post them on **your own channels**.
- Agree payment terms directly. Kreatli **does not process payments or hold escrow**; jobs list payment preferences such as PayPal, bank transfer, or crypto.

Posting a job is free. You do the hiring and managing; there is no account manager in the middle.

## When a clipping agency is the better choice

Be honest about your situation. An agency is often the right call when:

- **You want distribution at scale.** Running a campaign across a large clipper network, with view tracking and fraud checks, is real work. A campaign agency already has the network and the process.
- **You have no time to manage anyone.** If you cannot write briefs, review clips, or give weekly feedback, a managed service fills that gap.
- **Several people approve every clip.** Brands with legal, marketing, and brand-safety reviews benefit from an account team that runs that process.
- **You need guaranteed capacity.** An agency can cover sick days, spikes, and turnover without you rehiring.
- **You want one invoice and one contract** covering strategy, production, and reporting.

If several of these apply and the budget fits, an agency can be worth the premium.

## When a marketplace is the better choice

Hiring directly on a clipping marketplace usually wins when:

- **You are a solo creator or small team** that needs a steady stream of clips, not a full campaign operation.
- **You want clips on your own channels** so subscribers and analytics stay with you.
- **You want to choose the person.** You see samples and pick a clipper who knows your niche, whether that is gaming, podcasts, or finance.
- **You want fast feedback.** Notes go straight to the clipper, not through an account manager.
- **Your budget is below agency minimums**, or you want to scale up and down week to week without a retainer.

The trade-off is that you take on the coordination: writing the brief, reviewing clips, agreeing payment terms in writing, and replacing a clipper if it does not work out.

## Comparing the costs

Agency pricing bundles management into the price, so it is usually higher than paying a clipper directly for the same clips. Campaign agencies price against views, with campaign minimums; production agencies price by package or retainer. Ask any agency for its minimum, what is included, and how views or deliverables are verified. The guide to [verifying clip views](/guides/how-to-verify-clip-views) lists the questions worth asking.

Direct hires price per clip or per period. Freelance clips typically run **$15 to $35** for simple talking-head or podcast edits and **$50 to $75 or more** for complex gaming or motion work. See [how much to pay clippers](/guides/how-much-to-pay-clippers) for sample budgets, or estimate a view-based campaign with the [clipping campaign cost calculator](/tools/clipping-campaign-cost-calculator).

## A middle path

Many creators start with one clipper hired directly and treat them like a small in-house team: a weekly batch, a style guide, and a shared feedback doc. If you later need large-scale distribution, you can add a campaign, run by an agency or by yourself, without giving up your own channel pipeline. If you plan to run one yourself, read [how to run a clipping campaign](/guides/how-to-run-a-clipping-campaign).

To try the direct route, [post a clipping job](/jobs/create) and review applications before you commit.

## FAQ

### What is a clipping agency?

A clipping agency is a company that manages clipping for creators and brands. Some run view-based campaigns through a network of clippers who post on their own accounts; others assign editors to produce clips for your own channels on a package or retainer.

### How much does a clipping agency cost?

It varies by agency and model. Campaign agencies usually price against verified views and set minimum budgets; Lumina, for example, lists a $5,000 minimum per campaign as of 2026. Production agencies charge packages or monthly retainers. Ask for minimums and what is included before signing.

### Is a clipping agency better than hiring a clipper directly?

It is better if you need large-scale distribution, have no time to manage clippers, or need a multi-step approval process. Hiring directly is usually cheaper and gives you more control if you want steady clips on your own channels and can handle briefs and feedback.

### Do clipping agencies post clips on my channel?

It depends on the type. Campaign agencies typically have clippers post on their own accounts to spread your content. Production agencies, like a clipper you hire directly, deliver finished clips for you to post on your own channels.

### Can I switch from an agency to hiring a clipper later?

Yes. Many creators move once they know their format and volume. Start by documenting your style, references, and weekly needs, then post a job and run a paid test clip with two or three candidates before ending the agency contract.
