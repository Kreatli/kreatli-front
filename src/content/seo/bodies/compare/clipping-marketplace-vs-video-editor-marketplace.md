# Clipping Marketplace vs. Video Editor Marketplace: What a Clipping Marketplace Is and How It Differs

A **clipping marketplace** is a platform built around one kind of work: turning long videos, livestreams, and podcasts into short vertical clips for YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels. A video editor marketplace covers every kind of editing, from long-form YouTube to ads and corporate video. If your goal is a steady flow of short-form clips, a clipping marketplace usually puts you in front of the right people faster.

## What is a clipping marketplace?

A clipping marketplace connects creators who have hours of footage with **clippers**: freelancers who find the best moments in that footage and cut them into short, captioned, vertical clips. The platform's job posts, profiles, and vocabulary are all built around that one workflow.

Typical clipping work includes:

- Cutting podcast episodes into 20 to 60 second highlight clips
- Pulling reactions and big plays out of Twitch or Kick VODs
- Turning long YouTube videos into a batch of Shorts
- Writing hooks, adding captions, and reframing footage to 9:16

Creators on a clipping marketplace usually hire for **volume and consistency**: the same clipper delivering clips every week, in a style that matches the channel. For a deeper definition, see [what is a clipping marketplace](/glossary/what-is-a-clipping-marketplace).

## The two kinds of clipping marketplace

People searching "clipping marketplace" run into two different models that share the same name. It helps to know which one you are looking at.

### Hiring marketplaces

A creator posts a job, clippers apply, and the creator hires one for an agreed rate. The clipper **delivers finished files to the creator**, who posts them on their own channels. Pay does not depend on how many views a clip gets. Kreatli Marketplace works this way.

### Campaign (CPM) marketplaces

A creator or brand funds a budget, and many clippers edit the footage and **post clips on their own accounts**. Clippers earn a rate per 1,000 views until the budget runs out. This is usually called a [clipping campaign](/glossary/what-is-a-clipping-campaign), and platforms like Whop Content Rewards run on this model. The [Kreatli vs. Whop Clips comparison](/compare/kreatli-vs-whop-clips) covers the trade-offs in detail.

Both models are legitimate. They solve different problems: hiring marketplaces give you a dedicated clip editor for your own channels, while campaign marketplaces spread your content across many outside accounts.

## What is a video editor marketplace?

A **video editor marketplace** is any platform where you can hire editors for any kind of video. General freelance sites like Fiverr and Upwork fall into this group. Clippers are on them, but they sit alongside freelancers who edit:

- Long-form YouTube videos and documentaries
- Ads, product videos, and brand campaigns
- Courses, webinars, and corporate training
- Weddings, events, and music videos
- Motion graphics and animation

That breadth is useful if you need several kinds of editing. It also means a search for "video editor" returns a wide mix of skills, and you do more of the filtering yourself.

## Key differences at a glance

| Factor | Clipping marketplace (hiring) | Clipping marketplace (CPM campaigns) | Video editor marketplace |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary output | Short clips from your long content | Short clips posted on clippers' accounts | Any kind of edit |
| Who posts the clip | You, on your channels | The clipper, on their accounts | You |
| How the editor is paid | Agreed rate (per project, hourly, monthly) | Per 1,000 views from a budget | Per gig, project, or hour |
| Typical buyer | Creators scaling Shorts, TikToks, Reels | Creators and brands wanting reach | Brands, agencies, all creators |
| Applicants show | Vertical clip samples | Their posting accounts and past views | Mixed portfolios |
| Shared vocabulary | Hooks, retention, captions, 9:16 | Views, CPM, payout caps | Varies by project |
| Best for | A dedicated clipper for your channels | Distribution across many accounts | Varied editing needs |

## What the difference looks like in practice

Say you run a weekly podcast and want 15 clips a week.

On a **general video editor marketplace**, a post titled "video editor needed" can draw long-form editors, motion designers, and agencies, each with a different idea of the job. You end up explaining what a vertical clip is, what a hook should do in the first second, and why captions matter, before you can compare anyone.

On a **clipping hiring marketplace**, applicants already work in that format. Their samples are vertical clips, they talk about moment selection and caption style, and the pay types in the job post fit recurring work. You spend your time judging taste and reliability instead of explaining the basics.

On a **campaign marketplace**, you would not hire one person at all. You would set a rate per 1,000 views, write content rules, and let many clippers post your footage.

## Which one should you use?

Choose a **clipping hiring marketplace** when:

- You want clips posted on **your own** channels
- You want one or two clippers who learn your style
- You prefer a predictable cost over view-based payouts

Choose a **CPM campaign marketplace** when:

- You want reach across many accounts you do not own
- You are comfortable paying for views rather than edits

Choose a **general video editor marketplace** when:

- You also need long-form edits, ads, or motion graphics
- You want to compare clipping with other editing services in one place

Many creators combine them: a long-form editor for flagship videos and a dedicated clipper for daily short-form output.

## FAQ

### What is a clipping marketplace?

A clipping marketplace is a platform where creators find clippers who turn long videos, streams, and podcasts into short vertical clips. Some clipping marketplaces are for hiring a clipper at an agreed rate, and others run view-based campaigns where clippers post on their own accounts.

### Is a clipping marketplace the same as a clipping campaign platform?

Not always. Campaign platforms pay clippers per 1,000 views for clips they post on their own accounts. Hiring marketplaces like Kreatli connect you with a clipper who delivers files to you for an agreed rate, and you post them yourself.

### Can I hire a clipper on a general video editor marketplace?

Yes. Many clippers offer services on general freelance sites. The difference is filtering: you will see more unrelated editing services, so you need a specific job post and a careful review of short-form samples.

### How do clippers get paid on Kreatli Marketplace?

The creator sets the pay type and rate in the job, and the creator and clipper agree payment directly using methods like PayPal, bank transfer, or crypto. Kreatli does not process payments or hold escrow, so agree terms in writing before work starts.

### Should I use a clipping marketplace or a video editor marketplace?

Use a clipping marketplace if most of your editing need is short-form clips from long content. Use a general video editor marketplace if you also need long-form edits, ads, or motion graphics from the same place.
