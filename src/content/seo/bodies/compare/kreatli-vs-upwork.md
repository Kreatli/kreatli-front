## Finding clipping jobs on Upwork: what clippers run into

Upwork is huge, and some creators do post clipping work there. Clippers who try it usually hit a few recurring issues.

### The job titles do not say "clipping"

Many short-form posts are titled "video editor," "short form editor," or "TikTok editor." You have to search several terms and read each post to find out whether it is clipping from long content or something else entirely, like ad editing or animation.

### Applying costs money

On Upwork, freelancers spend **Connects** to submit proposals. As of 2026, Connects cost $0.15 each, and the number needed varies by job. That adds up when you are applying to many small clipping posts, and it pushes you to be selective before you have reviews.

### Fees come out of every contract

As of 2026, Upwork charges freelancers a **variable service fee from 0% to 15%**, set per contract and shown before you submit a proposal. On a modest per-clip rate, that cut is noticeable.

### You compete with every video editor

A clipping post on Upwork can draw proposals from long-form editors, agencies, and motion designers. A newer clipper with strong vertical samples can get lost in that pile.

None of this rules Upwork out. It means you should treat it as one channel among several rather than your only source of clipping work.

## Hiring a clipper on Upwork: what creators run into

For creators, Upwork's strength is also its friction:

- **Lots of proposals, mixed fit.** A post for "video editor" can bring dozens of proposals, many from editors who do not specialize in short-form.
- **A longer hiring cycle.** Writing a brief, reviewing proposals, interviewing, and setting up a contract can take days before the first clip arrives.
- **Platform fees on your side too.** Clients pay a marketplace fee on top of what goes to the freelancer, so check Upwork's current client pricing.

In return, Upwork processes payments, offers contract tools like time tracking for hourly work and milestones for fixed-price work, and has a large global pool.

## Where Upwork still makes sense

Upwork remains a reasonable option when:

- You need one contractor for **several skills**, like editing, motion graphics, and scripting
- You want payments, contracts, and time tracking **handled by the platform**
- Your project is **not mainly clipping**, like long-form documentary or ad editing
- You are a clipper who also does other editing and wants **broader client reach**

Kreatli Marketplace is built around short-form clipping, so the jobs you see are the jobs you want.

## How Kreatli works for creators

Creators post a job describing the content type, weekly volume, platforms, style, and turnaround, then choose a pay type and rate. Clippers with short-form portfolios apply, you shortlist, run a paid test clip, and hire. Because the pool is focused on clipping, you spend less time filtering out unrelated editing services.

## The trade-offs

Kreatli is narrower than Upwork on purpose. Know what that means before you switch:

- **No escrow or platform payments.** Creator and clipper agree payment terms directly. Put scope, revisions, and payment timing in writing.
- **No built-in time tracker.** If you work hourly, agree how hours are logged.
- **Clipping only.** If you also sell long-form or motion design work, Upwork may still be worth keeping.

## Which should you use?

**Clippers:** use Kreatli as your main source of clipping jobs if you want short-form clients without paying to apply. Keep Upwork if you also sell other editing services and can afford Connects.

**Creators:** use Upwork if you want platform-managed contracts and payments or need several skills from one person. Use Kreatli if you want a clipper for your channels and prefer a shorter path from job post to first clips. [Post a clipping job](/jobs/create) to get started.

## FAQ

### Are there clipping jobs on Upwork?

Yes, but they are often listed as "video editor" or "short form editor" jobs alongside every other kind of video work. You need to search several terms and read each post to find true clipping work, and each proposal costs Connects.

### What is the best Upwork alternative for clippers?

A clipping-focused marketplace like Kreatli Marketplace is the closest alternative. Creators post short-form clipping jobs with the pay type stated, and clippers sign up and apply for free.

### How much does Upwork charge freelancers?

As of 2026, Upwork charges freelancers a variable service fee of 0% to 15% per contract, and Connects used to submit proposals cost $0.15 each. Upwork changes its pricing from time to time, so check its current fee pages.

### Do I need Connects or credits to apply on Kreatli?

No. Clippers can sign up and apply to clipping jobs for free. Premium and points are optional ways to boost visibility, not a requirement to apply.

### Can I use Upwork and Kreatli at the same time?

Yes. Many freelancers keep profiles on more than one platform. A common setup is to use Kreatli for clipping work and Upwork for other editing projects, keeping your portfolio consistent on both.
