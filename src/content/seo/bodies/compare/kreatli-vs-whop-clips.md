# Whop Clipping vs Kreatli: Content Rewards or Hiring a Clipper?

**Whop clipping vs Kreatli** comes down to what you pay for. Whop Content Rewards pays clippers per 1,000 views on clips they post to their own accounts, while Kreatli Marketplace lets creators hire a clipper at an agreed rate to deliver finished clips for the creator's own channels.

This page is a direct two-way comparison. For the wider field, including other CPM platforms, Discord servers, and agencies, see [Whop Content Rewards alternatives](/alternatives/whop-content-rewards-alternatives). If you're a clipper wanting the step-by-step, read [how Whop clipping works](/guides/whop-clipping).

## How Whop Content Rewards works

Whop's documentation and Content Rewards terms describe the model as follows, as of 2026:

- The business running the campaign **funds a budget** and sets a **reward rate per 1,000 views**.
- It can add an optional **flat fee per approved submission** on top of the view-based reward.
- A **minimum payout** per video decides which submissions enter the review queue, and a **maximum payout** caps what a single video can earn.
- Clippers post to **their own social accounts** on the platforms the business accepts, such as TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and X.
- The business **approves or rejects** each submission against its stated requirements, and Whop pays approved clips based on their views.
- Approved clips are paid first come, first served until the campaign's payout limit is reached or its end date passes. Views Whop attributes to bots or automation are excluded.
- Whop's terms set a **10% Content Reward Fee** on the amounts a business pays out to clippers, charged to the business.

In short, the business buys **views on other people's accounts**, with Whop handling tracking and payouts. If CPM is new to you, read [what CPM means in clipping](/glossary/cpm-meaning-clipping).

## How Kreatli Marketplace works

**Kreatli Marketplace** is a clipping marketplace where creators (streamers, YouTubers, podcasters, and brands) hire clippers directly.

- A creator posts a **clipping job** with deliverables, skills, availability (full-time, part-time, or project-based), and a pay type: **per project, hourly, monthly, or yearly**.
- Clippers apply with a cover letter or list their services. Clipper profiles are reviewed by the Kreatli team.
- The clipper delivers **finished files**, and the creator posts them on **their own channels**.
- Pay is agreed upfront and **does not depend on views**.
- Kreatli **does not process payments or hold escrow**. Jobs list payment preferences such as PayPal, bank transfer, or crypto, and creator and clipper settle terms directly.

Joining and posting jobs is free. Clippers can use an optional Premium subscription or points to boost their visibility.

## Side-by-side comparison

| Factor | Whop Content Rewards | Kreatli Marketplace |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What the creator pays for | Approved views, plus an optional flat fee per approved clip | The clipping work itself |
| Pay basis | Rate per 1,000 views set by the business | Per project, hourly, monthly, or yearly |
| Where clips are posted | Clippers' own accounts | The creator's own channels |
| Who does the work | Any clipper who joins and gets approved | A clipper you pick after reviewing samples |
| Output | Whatever the campaign attracts and you approve | Agreed deliverables and weekly volume |
| Cost controls | Budget, rate, and max payout per video | Agreed price per clip, project, or period |
| Platform fee | 10% Content Reward Fee charged to the business | Free to post jobs |
| Payment handling | Whop pays approved views | Arranged directly between creator and clipper |
| Clipper needs | Posting accounts that get views | A portfolio and the ability to win jobs |

## The core difference: whose channel grows

With **Whop clipping**, every clip lives on a clipper's account. Your content reaches audiences you do not own, and the followers and watch history build up on those accounts. That is useful for awareness, launches, and sponsor pushes, where the goal is to be seen in as many feeds as possible.

With **Kreatli**, the clipper hands you the files. The views, followers, and analytics accrue to **your** Shorts, TikTok, and Reels accounts. You also get a fixed number of clips each week from someone who learns your hooks, pacing, and running jokes.

Rights also work differently. Whop's terms give Whop and the business a broad, perpetual license to use campaign clips. On a marketplace hire, usage rights are part of the deal you agree with the clipper, so put them in writing alongside scope and pay.

## What each model costs a creator

**Whop:** total spend is roughly views paid, divided by 1,000, times your rate, plus any flat fees, plus the 10% fee. A $1,000 payout budget at $1 per 1,000 views buys up to about 1 million approved views, if clippers deliver them. You control the ceiling, not the floor: a campaign that attracts weak clips may underspend, and a hot one can hit its cap quickly. Model a budget with the [clipping campaign cost calculator](/tools/clipping-campaign-cost-calculator).

**Kreatli:** you pay for clips, not views. Freelance clips typically run **$15 to $35** for simple talking-head or podcast edits and **$50 to $75 or more** for complex gaming or motion work, with retainers often landing a little lower per clip. See [how much to pay clippers](/guides/how-much-to-pay-clippers) for sample budgets.

## Which pays clippers better?

It depends on your accounts. On Whop, a clip that clears the minimum and takes off can earn far more than a flat fee, but a clip under the minimum earns nothing, and a campaign can run out of budget before your submission is approved. You also need posting accounts that reliably get views.

On Kreatli, you are paid the agreed rate whether the clip goes viral or not, and a creator who likes your work tends to come back weekly. The trade-offs are no viral upside and the need to win jobs. Compare a CPM month against a fixed-rate month with the [clipping earnings calculator](/tools/clipping-earnings-calculator).

## When Whop Content Rewards is the better choice

- You want **reach across many outside accounts**, not clips on your own channel.
- You are promoting a launch, product, or sponsor and care about total views.
- You accept variable output and are willing to review submissions.
- As a clipper, you already have accounts that get consistent views.

## When Kreatli is the better choice

- You want **clips on your own channels** at a set weekly volume.
- You want one clipper who learns your style, not a rotating crowd.
- You prefer a known cost per clip or per month.
- As a clipper, you want **predictable pay** and repeat clients without growing posting accounts.

## Can you use both?

Yes. Some creators run a Whop campaign for distribution while a hired clipper feeds their own channels. Many clippers do the same in reverse: fixed-pay clients set an income floor, and CPM campaigns add upside. Keep the two separate, since a client's clips belong on the client's channels, not in a campaign submission.

## FAQ

### Is Whop clipping better than hiring a clipper?

Neither is better in general. Whop Content Rewards buys views on clippers' own accounts, which suits distribution campaigns. Hiring a clipper on Kreatli buys finished clips for your own channels, which suits creators who want steady output and growth on accounts they own.

### Who pays the Whop Content Rewards fee?

As of 2026, Whop's Content Rewards terms set a 10% fee on amounts the business pays out to clippers, charged to the business, which must hold enough funds to cover payouts plus fees. Check Whop's current terms before budgeting, since fees can change.

### Does Kreatli pay clippers per view?

No. On Kreatli Marketplace the creator and clipper agree pay upfront, per project, hourly, monthly, or yearly. Kreatli does not process the payment; the two sides settle it directly using the payment preference listed on the job.

### Do I need followers to earn on Whop or Kreatli?

On Whop, you post clips to your own accounts and are paid on approved views, so accounts that get views matter a lot. On Kreatli, you deliver files to the creator, so your samples and reliability matter more than your follower count.

### Can I clip for Whop campaigns and Kreatli clients at the same time?

Yes, as long as you follow each campaign's rules and keep client work separate. Clips you make for a hired client belong to that client's channels and should not be submitted to a Whop campaign unless the client agrees.
