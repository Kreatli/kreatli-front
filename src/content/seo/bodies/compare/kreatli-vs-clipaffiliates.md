# ClipAffiliates vs Kreatli: Two Ways to Hire Clippers

**ClipAffiliates vs Kreatli** is really a question of what "hiring a clipper" means to you. On ClipAffiliates, a brand funds a CPM campaign and pays any clipper whose posts earn verified views; on Kreatli Marketplace, a creator hires a specific clipper at an agreed rate to deliver finished clips for the creator's own channels.

Both are legitimate. They buy different things, and they cost money in different ways.

## How ClipAffiliates works

ClipAffiliates describes itself as a clipping and UGC marketplace connecting brands with clippers. Based on its own site and brand guides, as of 2026:

- A brand creates a campaign, uploads **source assets**, sets a **CPM** (pay per 1,000 views), and deposits a **budget**.
- Clippers join the campaign, post clips to **their own accounts**, and earn per **verified view**. ClipAffiliates says views are verified through platform APIs before anyone is paid.
- Brands can set a **max payout per video** and a **minimum view threshold**, and approve or reject clips during a review window.
- ClipAffiliates lists a **9% platform fee on brand deposits**, with no subscription. A $1,000 budget costs $1,090 with the fee.
- It also supports **UGC campaigns**, where creators make original content rather than clips.
- Its own guides put typical clipper pay at roughly **$1 to $5 per 1,000 verified views**, with niches like finance and crypto at the higher end.

It is a self-serve marketplace: the brand writes the brief, sets the rules, and reviews submissions. Check ClipAffiliates' current terms before budgeting, since fees and settings can change.

## How Kreatli Marketplace works

**Kreatli Marketplace** is a clipping marketplace for hiring clippers directly.

- A creator posts a **clipping job** with deliverables, skills, availability, and a pay type: **per project, hourly, monthly, or yearly**.
- Clippers apply with a cover letter or list their services. Clipper profiles are reviewed by the Kreatli team.
- The clipper delivers **finished files**, and the creator posts them on their own channels.
- Pay is agreed upfront and **does not depend on views**.
- Kreatli **does not process payments or hold escrow**. Jobs list payment preferences such as PayPal, bank transfer, or crypto, and the two sides settle directly.

Joining and posting jobs is free. Clippers can boost visibility with an optional Premium subscription or points.

## What a clipping campaign costs vs hiring a clipper

The two models are priced on different units, so compare them on what you actually want.

**A CPM campaign on ClipAffiliates** costs roughly your CPM times the views you want, divided by 1,000, plus the platform fee. A campaign at $2 per 1,000 views aiming for 500,000 verified views needs a $1,000 budget, or $1,090 with the 9% fee. Your max payout per video and minimum view threshold shape how that budget spreads across clippers. The [clipping campaign cost calculator](/tools/clipping-campaign-cost-calculator) runs these numbers for you.

**Hiring a clipper on Kreatli** costs a rate per clip, project, or period. Freelance clips typically run **$15 to $35** for simple talking-head or podcast edits and **$50 to $75 or more** for complex gaming or motion work. Twenty simple clips a month at $25 is $500, and every one of them lands on your channel. See [how much to pay clippers](/guides/how-much-to-pay-clippers) for more sample budgets.

Neither is cheaper in the abstract. A CPM campaign buys reach you could not get from your own accounts. A hire buys a predictable stream of clips that grow channels you own.

## Where ClipAffiliates fits better

A CPM campaign is the stronger choice when:

- You are a **brand or product** that wants to appear in many feeds, not grow one channel.
- You can define what a good clip looks like in a brief and review submissions.
- You want to pay only for verified views, and variable output is acceptable.
- You want UGC-style content alongside clips.

To plan one well, read [how to run a clipping campaign](/guides/how-to-run-a-clipping-campaign).

## Where Kreatli fits better

Hiring directly is the stronger choice when:

- You are a **creator** who wants Shorts, TikToks, and Reels on your own channels every week.
- You want one clipper who learns your hooks, pacing, and audience.
- You need a set number of clips by a set date.
- You want a cost you can plan around rather than a cost tied to views.

The [step-by-step guide to hiring a clipper](/guides/how-to-hire-a-clipper) covers scoping, job posts, paid test clips, and onboarding.

## For clippers choosing between them

On ClipAffiliates, as on any CPM platform, your income depends on views. Accounts that already get reach earn the most, and a strong edit that underperforms can earn little. On Kreatli, you are paid for the edit itself, so a portfolio matters more than a following, but you need to win jobs and there is no viral upside. Plenty of clippers do both.

## FAQ

### Is ClipAffiliates a way to hire a clipper?

In a sense. On ClipAffiliates you fund a campaign and any clipper who joins can post and earn on verified views. That is different from hiring one clipper to deliver clips for your channel, which is what Kreatli Marketplace is built for.

### How much does a clipping campaign cost on ClipAffiliates?

It depends on your CPM and the views you want. As of 2026, ClipAffiliates lists a 9% fee on brand deposits, so a $1,000 payout budget costs $1,090. Its guides cite typical clipper pay of about $1 to $5 per 1,000 verified views. Check its current terms before budgeting.

### Is hiring a clipper cheaper than running a clipping campaign?

They buy different things. A campaign buys views on other people's accounts, while a hired clipper produces clips for your own channels at a fixed rate. If owned-channel growth is the goal, hiring is usually easier to budget. If broad reach is the goal, a campaign may deliver more views per dollar.

### Does Kreatli take a fee from campaign budgets?

No. Kreatli does not run CPM campaigns or process payments. Posting a job is free, and creators pay clippers directly using the payment method listed on the job, such as PayPal, bank transfer, or crypto.

### Can I use ClipAffiliates and Kreatli together?

Yes. Some brands and creators run a CPM campaign for reach while a hired clipper keeps their own channels supplied. Keep the briefs separate so campaign clippers and your hired clipper are not cutting the same moments.
